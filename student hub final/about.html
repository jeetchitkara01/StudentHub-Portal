About StudentHub

StudentHub is an integrated academic portal developed to streamline student communications, course catalogs, performance tracking, and event registrations.

Our Mission

To provide students with easy, 24/7 digital access to academic resources, circulars, grades, and campus opportunities.

Our Vision

To create a modern, paperless, and accessible digital campus environment for all students and faculty.

Key Objectives

  1. Centralized notices and real-time academic announcements.
  2. Online semester results lookup with CGPA calculation.
  3. Direct registration for campus hackathons and technical fests.
